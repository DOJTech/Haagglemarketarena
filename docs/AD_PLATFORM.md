# Sponsored price tests: what Haggle is, and is not

**Accurate description:** Haggle is an early **sponsored price-test platform** built on a game. It is **not an ad exchange**. Do not call it one.

## What exists (built and tested)
| Piece | How it works |
|---|---|
| Ad serving | An approved sponsor's brand and product name appear in the game's sponsored slot. One slot, one campaign at a time (several live campaigns rotate between games). |
| Brand safety | Allowed categories only (food, non-alcoholic drinks, household, personal care, phone accessories, fashion, education, general services). Blocked terms (alcohol, tobacco, betting, loans, crypto, adult, weapons, health "cure" claims and similar). Language and phone-number checks. Every campaign starts **pending** and goes live only after you approve it. You can pause or end it at any moment. Players are 13 and over, so the rules are deliberately strict. |
| Fair ranking | Ranked games show the brand but never change any price. Price tests run in **practice games only**, so sponsors cannot distort the leaderboard. |
| Price tests | Each practice game is randomly assigned to one price group (for example wholesale price x0.9, x1.0, x1.1 for the sponsored product). Players decide how much to buy and what to sell it for. |
| Reporting | Owner Metrics tab, and a private **brand report link** per campaign (unguessable address, one campaign only, no player data). A group shows numbers only after 30 finished games. Each report opens with a plain-language warning that results are from a game. |
| Invoicing record | You record the agreed fee and mark the campaign Invoiced or Paid. **Payment itself happens outside the app** (bank transfer, or a Paystack or Flutterwave payment link sent by you). |

## What does NOT exist (do not claim it)
- No auction or bidding, no advertiser self-service sign-up, no publishers other than Haggle.
- No automated payments to or from advertisers.
- No third-party ad verification, and no real shopper or sales data.
- Results are about how **traders in a game** react to a price. They do not predict real sales until a pilot checks them (see `PILOT_PLAYBOOK.md`).

## To grow toward an exchange later (each is a separate project)
Advertiser accounts and sign-in, automated billing (needs a registered business and a payment provider's approval), many ad slots and bidding rules, independent ad verification, and a data-protection review.

## Run a first sponsor pilot
1. Find one small business with a product that fits the allowed categories.
2. Agree in writing: what is tested, for how long, the fee (or free pilot), and that results come from a game.
3. In Metrics, open **Sponsor campaigns**, create the campaign, and tap Approve.
4. Share the **Copy report link** with the business. Reports show numbers once 30 games per group are finished.
5. Invoice outside the app, then mark Invoiced and Paid.

## Safe wording
"Haggle is an early sponsored price-test platform. Brands can run controlled price tests in a game and see how traders react. I am looking for first pilot partners."
