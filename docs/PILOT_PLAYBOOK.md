# Checking game results against real sales (the only way to earn the claim)

Until this is done, say: "results show how traders react in a game." Never say "predicts real sales."

1. **Pick a partner** with a product sold in several real outlets or markets, and who will share simple sales numbers.
2. **Run a real price change** for 2 to 4 weeks. Best: two prices in different outlets at the same time. Next best: one outlet, two periods.
3. **Run the same test in Haggle** (same price ratio, as a campaign price group) until each group has at least 100 finished games.
4. **Agree before you look** what counts as a match, for example: both show that a lower price raises units, and both rank the price options in the same order.
5. **Compare** direction and rank order first. Size of effect only if the direction matches.
6. **Publish honestly,** including a mismatch. One match is not proof. Wait for at least 3 pilots before saying "predicts".
7. **Protect people:** share only totals with the partner and never player data; keep the partner's sales numbers confidential.
