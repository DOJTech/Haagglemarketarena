# What changed in v2.3 (game, music, currency)

**Game rules (version 3).** Old unfinished games show "that saved game is from an older version" and the player starts a new one. Old leaderboard scores came from different rules, so they are not comparable; before launch, clear them (or keep them as history).
- 12 goods (7 new, tied to weather), 6 kinds of weather with a forecast that is right 3 days in 4.
- 7 to 10 shoppers a day (weather and reputation), limited selling time each day.
- 5 shopper personalities with hints (about 1 in 7 bluffs), shoppers who want 2 units, near-miss deals, big spenders who walk out when insulted.
- Fixed: the old shopper reply let a careful player work out the exact limit.
- Scoring recalibrated by simulation (profit target 45,000, margin 10 to 50 percent).

**Honest results of the design testing** (simulated bots, not people)
- Beginner-style bot about 365 of 1000; a decent heuristic about 545; a bot that knows every limit about 890.
- Hints are right 88% of the time and shift what shoppers pay by about 10 to 25% (big spenders about 124% of the usual range, bargain hunters about 91%).
- Tailoring a bot's asking strategy to the hint gained only about 3 to 4% extra profit. The depth comes mainly from stock planning under an uncertain forecast, the time limit, patience and reputation. Real players are the real test: watch where they struggle and adjust the numbers at the top of `engine.js`.

**Music.** Built-in original beats (mood follows weather), your own files from your phone, internet links (direct audio, radio streams, Spotify, YouTube). No commercial songs are stored or supplied. The sound was generated by code that I could not listen to: please test on real phones and tell me what to change (tempo, volume, instruments).

**Currency.** 11 display currencies, suggested from the country. Rates are rough fixed numbers in `public/common.js` (`CUR`); update them whenever you like.

**Not run here:** the older integration suites (anticheat, features, moderation, campaigns, security, load) and the simulated-browser suite against a real server and database. They were written for the old rules, so some may need updates; GitHub's automatic checks will show which.
