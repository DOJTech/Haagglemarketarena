HAGGLE MARKET ARENA

Haggle Market Arena is a simulation. No real money changes hands, and results do not predict real markets.

About, privacy and your data

What is stored on our server: a random device ID, your display name, your country (optional), your games and scores, and a short signal that you are playing right now. We do not ask for your real name, phone number or email.

What is public: your display name, country and score appear on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name.

On this device: your settings and best score are saved in your browser. Clearing browser data may erase them.

Reset this device clears saved data on this device only. Delete my data also deletes your records from our server. Copies kept in backups by our hosting providers may take longer to disappear.

Currencies are for display only. The game runs in naira, and amounts are shown at fixed, approximate exchange rates, not live rates.
