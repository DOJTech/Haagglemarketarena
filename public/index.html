Music

Works with a direct link to a song file or radio stream, or a Spotify or YouTube link (shown in that service's own player).

Haggle does not supply or store commercial songs. To hear the latest Nigerian hits, use your own files, or open your music app (Audiomack, Boomplay, Spotify, Apple Music and others) and let it play in the background while you play. Music never starts by itself.

Haggle Market Arena is a simulation. No real money changes hands, and results do not predict real markets.

About, privacy and your data

What is stored on our server: a random device ID, your display name, your country (optional), your games and scores, and a short signal that you are playing right now. We do not ask for your real name, phone number or email. If you create an account, we also store your private username, a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password, and your sign-in sessions. We cannot see your password.

What is public: your display name, country and score appear on the leaderboard. Do not use your real name.

On this device: your settings and best score are saved in your browser. Clearing browser data may erase them.

Reset this device clears saved data on this device only. Delete my data also deletes your records from our server. Copies kept in backups by our hosting providers may take longer to disappear.
